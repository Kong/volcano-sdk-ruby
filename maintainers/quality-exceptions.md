The exact-method `@dynamic` annotations preserve the public RBS types and consumer checks. | [Steep's documented dynamic-method annotation](https://github.com/soutaro/steep#2-write-ruby-code); removing both annotations produces exactly ten `Ruby::MethodDefinitionMissing` diagnostics under `bundle exec steep check --jobs 1`, while the annotated target has no diagnostics. |
