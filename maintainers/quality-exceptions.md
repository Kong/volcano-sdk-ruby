# Quality exceptions

The exact Steep method scopes are in [steep-dynamic-methods.json](steep-dynamic-methods.json).
Native regression checks require every listed annotation to exist and to produce
its missing-method diagnostic when removed from a temporary source copy.

A `**options` alternative produced six Steep parameter-kind/type diagnostics and violated the method-length limit. The exception spec reenables the cop and requires exactly this diagnostic. |
